# Distribution review

## Included in the initial Private release

Author-created offline research code, method documentation, synthetic examples/tests, source hashes, aggregate candidate-flow and layer-yield counts, and clearly marked paper adaptations. No original Git history is imported. A file-by-file publication whitelist governs Git staging, with content scans before the first commit.

## Excluded and retained in the original archive

Licensed CRSP/Compustat/CCM raw and derived data, daily market/signal matrices, complete historical candidate expressions/registries, BRAIN Alpha IDs, individual platform outcomes, original unredacted papers and supplementary tables, screenshots, platform ledgers, manual-review workbooks, browser sessions, cookies, account identifiers, credentials and all unrelated coursework/personal files. The precise local exclusion inventory is outside the Git repository.

WRDS's official [data-sharing guidance](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/about/navigating-co-authorship-across-institutions-a-guide-to-data-sharing-and-compliance/) distinguishes shareable code/documentation from restricted raw data sharing. The [WRDS Terms of Use](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/users/tou/) and your institution/vendor agreements apply. Private visibility does not authorize licensed redistribution.

The public [WorldQuant website terms](https://www.worldquant.com/terms-and-conditions/) and [BRAIN privacy policy](https://platform.worldquantbrain.com/agreements/privacy-policy) do not establish the author's rights under a specific BRAIN user, consulting or competition agreement. The relevant accepted agreement and any confidential-information/IP obligations were not available for this release review. Candidate-level expressions and platform evidence are therefore withheld. No claim that public website terms authorize those records is made.

## Remaining decisions

1. The author must approve Public visibility after checking whether their applicable agreements also constrain the released aggregate discussion and general research code. Technical scans are not a legal rights determination.
2. The author may choose to pin the repository on their profile.
3. The author must select a LICENSE, including scope and any third-party rights, before one is added. No MIT or other blanket open-source permission is granted by this release.

Private pushing of this filtered release is authorized by the publication task. Adding withheld items, changing visibility and adding a license are separate actions. No repository or profile outside this project is modified.
