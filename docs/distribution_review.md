# Distribution review

## Included in the reviewed release

Author-created offline research code, method documentation, synthetic examples/tests, source hashes, aggregate candidate-flow and layer-yield counts, and clearly marked paper adaptations. No original Git history is imported. A file-by-file publication whitelist governs Git staging, with content scans before the first commit.

## Excluded and retained in the original archive

Licensed CRSP/Compustat/CCM raw and derived data, daily market/signal matrices, complete historical candidate expressions/registries, BRAIN Alpha IDs, individual platform outcomes, original unredacted papers and supplementary tables, screenshots, platform ledgers, manual-review workbooks, browser sessions, cookies, account identifiers, credentials and all unrelated coursework/personal files. The precise local exclusion inventory is outside the Git repository.

WRDS's official [data-sharing guidance](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/pages/about/navigating-co-authorship-across-institutions-a-guide-to-data-sharing-and-compliance/) distinguishes shareable code/documentation from restricted raw data sharing. The [WRDS Terms of Use](https://wrds-www.wharton.upenn.edu/users/tou/) and your institution/vendor agreements apply. Private visibility does not authorize licensed redistribution.

## BRAIN agreement review, 2026-10-10

The author confirmed an ordinary research account. The accepted WorldQuant BRAIN User Agreement, Terms and Conditions, and Challenge Guidelines were read through the authenticated account's agreement viewer. A pending Base Fee Calculation document was not accepted. Account identifiers, screenshots and the full agreement text are not redistributed here.

The [BRAIN User Agreement](https://platform.worldquantbrain.com/agreements/user-agreement), section 2, grants users a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use, copy, distribute and adapt their own platform Output. Section 3 licenses the author's Input to the company non-exclusively. Sections 5 and 7 preserve platform ownership and restrict disclosure of the platform and its elements; those restrictions do not authorize copying platform code, data feeds, documentation or screenshots. The website Terms' final Miscellaneous provision gives a capability-specific agreement priority for that capability in a conflict. The accepted Challenge Guidelines incorporate the User Agreement; no additional confidentiality restriction on these released aggregates was identified.

This review supports publication of the author's offline code and discussion of the author's own aggregate simulation outputs. This is a scope assessment from the displayed agreements, not a claim of ownership of the platform or a grant of rights to vendor data. The [privacy policy](https://platform.worldquantbrain.com/agreements/privacy-policy) alone is not the permission basis. The released equations and methods are author-written mathematical descriptions, not copied platform software. All charts are author-drawn. There are no platform screenshots or vendor stock-level rows in this release.

The current whitelist retains the existing display adaptations and aggregate evidence. Withheld candidate-level registries, exact expressions, individual platform records and original unredacted papers remain in the original private archive; they were not automatically added following this agreement review. Adding any such material requires a separate file-by-file provenance and rights review. Their exclusion here is not a conclusion that all user-generated expressions are prohibited from publication.

## Remaining decisions

1. The author explicitly authorized Public visibility conditional on the security and agreement review. That review supports the current filtered release. New data or archived evidence requires a fresh content and rights review.
2. The author may choose to pin the repository on their profile.
3. The author must select a LICENSE, including scope and any third-party rights, before one is added. No MIT or other blanket open-source permission is granted by this release.

The publication task authorizes normal commits and Public visibility for this filtered repository. It does not authorize a history rewrite, force push, blanket third-party licensing or publication of licensed data. No repository or profile outside this project is modified.
